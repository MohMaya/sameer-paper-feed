### Two ships where 125 used to sail

*Kpler counted two commodity vessels on Monday. Brent November slipped to about $99.70.*

Commodity traffic through the Strait of Hormuz fell to two vessels on Monday from ten on Sunday, preliminary Kpler data showed. Before the Iran conflict began on 28 February the strait typically handled about 125 large commercial ships a day. A minerals-laden Panama-flagged Supramax and a Liberia-flagged bulker made the crossings. Over the weekend the Isle of Man-flagged crude tanker LR Stephanie and the Liberia-flagged LPG carrier Al Maryah were struck by unidentified projectiles; both continued without tow. President Donald Trump said he would “probably” meet Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian at the United Nations. IRGC commander Ahmad Mohebbi warned of “new weapons.” Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said Iranian airlines face fuel and landing cutoffs from Wednesday. A strait that moves a fifth of the world’s oil and gas trade on two ships is not a negotiation table. It is a rationing list.

Chinese President Xi Jinping arrives in Washington on Wednesday for a state visit that includes Thursday talks with President Trump and a Friday stop at the National Archives. ### Maharashtra left 2.07 crore names off the draft

*Twenty-point-six-nine million of 97.85 million electors, or 21.14 percent, did not make the draft roll.*

Maharashtra’s Special Intensive Revision draft excludes 2.07 crore names from a roll of 9.785 crore. Of those, 34.8 lakh are marked deceased, 1.548 crore shifted or absent, and 17.7 lakh as multiples. Claims and objections run to 30 September. The final roll is due on 4 November. Congress called the exclusion a method of disenfranchisement. In Delhi, The Hindu’s data desk found that 24 of 70 assembly constituencies now show fewer electors than the turnout recorded in the 2025 assembly polls. The Supreme Court upheld the Commission’s power to run a special intensive revision in May. A cleansing that removes one in five names in a state the size of Maharashtra is either overdue hygiene or a second franchise. The booth will decide which sentence fits.

### Merz held the coalition and skipped Turtle Bay

*AfD took 38.2 percent in Mecklenburg-Western Pomerania. The CDU finished on 4.9 percent and missed the Landtag.*

Chancellor Friedrich Merz’s Christian Democrats collapsed to 4.9 percent in Mecklenburg-Western Pomerania on Sunday and failed the five-percent threshold, a postwar first. The Alternative for Germany finished first at 38.2 percent, ahead of the SPD on 35.5 percent. In Berlin, Die Linke led with 25.7 percent; the CDU took 18.8 percent and the AfD 16.3 percent. Merz vowed to keep the ruling coalition together, skipped the UN General Assembly, and left the firewall against the AfD formally intact. His approval rating sits near 13 percent. National polls put the AfD around 30 percent. A chancellor who holds the arithmetic and loses the map is still a chancellor. He is no longer the story Europe’s centre expected to tell.

*India made 216 for 3 and beat Sri Lanka by 147 runs. Mandhana finished 79 off 45.*
